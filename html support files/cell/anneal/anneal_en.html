Skybridge check logic
Bridge Score = 35% Distance + 25% Height + 25% Intensity + 15% Size
Pairs are tested only within one site. Once a pair passes the threshold, the check goes on to maximum clear span, skybridges per building, collision with a third building and clashes with skybridges at the same height. Building use intensity is stood in for by estimated gross floor area for now. Skybridges are a post-processing analysis and do not enter the original evaluate() annealing score function.
